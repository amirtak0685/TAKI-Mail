# TAKI mail
#### Description: TAKI Mail is web based application, in which users can work with their emails and perform multiple actions with them, much similar to Gmail, and many other known email apps.
When users login, or new users register, they have three items in the menu: compose, inbox and sent. In inbox, users view all the details of their recieved emails. In compose, users send emails to other users on the website. And in sent, users view all the details of emails they have sent. Users can also reply on the emails they have sent.
It was hard deciding what parts to put in the project, because email apps are very complicated today. But I decided to to go with the basics, and not include the more twisted parts of todays email apps. The only hard part of the project for me was the reply part, in which I had to figure out a way to keep all the email details except the body, and connecting it to the sender and recipient through the database. Eventually I managed to figure it out, but it was quite breathtaking!
I built the app much similar to finance in pset9, I used the same files and changed the functions, but the idea of both applications is the same. I needed a strong database, much stronger than finance, because emails are mostly database, but finance mostly needed a live source for the price of the shares. I also needed to provide all the data by myself, because I wasn't using an API.
Overall, I'm very proud of what the project turned out to be, because I feel it is a strong basic version of big email apps. I hope to carry the knowledge I learned during this project, and during all of CS50, into my day to day life and program many complex applications.
